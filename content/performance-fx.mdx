# Performance FX

Performance FX are for playing the machine live. They sit after sound design, sequencing, and arrangement, giving you a way to transform the groove while it runs.

The FX layer answers this question:

How should the music be performed right now?

## FX page

Open FX from the Home menu to choose and control effects.

Effects can be global or track-focused depending on the current design. The important idea is that FX are meant to be immediate. They should be reachable during playback without interrupting the groove.

## Filter

Filter is the most basic performance effect.

Use it to:

- Remove high frequencies for a darker section.
- Sweep into a drop.
- Thin out a busy pattern.
- Emphasize a transition.

A filter move is often the safest first performance gesture because it changes the energy without changing the notes.

## Stutter

Stutter repeats a short slice of audio or sequencer output.

Use stutter for:

- Fills at the end of a phrase.
- Rhythmic glitches.
- Fast repeats before a drop.
- Short live variations without editing the pattern.

Stutter is most effective when used briefly. Let the groove return before the gesture becomes distracting.

## Vinyl and scratch effects

Vinyl-style effects can create stops, slowdowns, pitch dips, or playback gestures.

Scratch-style effects can create more aggressive movement, especially on drums, samples, or full mix moments.

Use these effects as performance punctuation. They work best at section boundaries, fills, and transitions.

## FX Banks

An FX Bank stores an effect setup or effect chain for fast recall.

Example banks:

- FX Bank 1: filter and delay.
- FX Bank 2: vinyl stop and reverb.
- FX Bank 3: stutter and bitcrush.
- FX Bank 4: scratch and tape wobble.
- FX Bank 5: glitch combo.

FX Banks let you prepare performance moves before the live moment. Instead of building an effect chain while the song plays, you recall the bank and perform it.

## Pattern automation vs Performance FX

FX automation belongs to the pattern. It repeats every time the pattern plays.

Performance FX belong to the live moment. They are used when you want to react, transition, or improvise.

Use both together: automate small repeatable motion, then use FX Banks for larger gestures.
